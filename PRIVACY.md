# Privacy

Tic Tac Toe Arena respects your privacy.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The web version makes no network requests, uses no cookies,
has no analytics and no account system.

## What stays on your device

Game progress (XP, level, achievements, statistics, settings) is stored
only in your browser's `localStorage` under the `ttta_` prefix. It never
leaves your device. Clearing your browser data removes it.

## On portal versions

When played on Poki or CrazyGames, those platforms may collect their own
telemetry under their respective privacy policies. This game's own code
behaves identically to the web version: no additional data is collected
or transmitted by the game itself.

## Children

The game contains no user-generated content, no chat, and no external
links, making it suitable for all audiences.
